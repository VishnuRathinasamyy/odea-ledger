Google Drive

Bills are uploaded through a small Google Apps Script that owns the bills folder. Files stay private.

  1. Open script.google.com with the company Google account and create a new project.
  2. Paste the contents of apps-script/Code.gs, then set the Script Properties FIREBASE_PROJECT_ID (and optionally ROOT_FOLDER_ID).
  3. Deploy → New deployment → Web app · Execute as Me · Who has access Anyone.
  4. Copy the /exec URL into driveProxyUrl in js/config.js and push to GitHub.
